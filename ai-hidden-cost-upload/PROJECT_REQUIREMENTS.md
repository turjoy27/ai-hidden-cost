
# Project: AI — The Hidden Cost

## Objective
Build an immersive, single-page educational website
investigating the environmental impact of artificial
intelligence and its potential influence on human creativity.

## Target Audience
General internet users.

## Design Direction
- Dark cinematic visual identity.
- Realistic Earth and water imagery.
- Deep blue, cyan and restrained green accents.
- Large editorial typography.
- Extensive but optimized animations.
- Responsive mobile and desktop layouts.

## Homepage Experience
- 3–5 second cinematic opening.
- Earth emerges from darkness.
- Water droplet animation.
- Main headline: Every AI Answer Has a Cost.
- Fixed navigation.
- Smooth scrolling storytelling.

## Section 1: Environmental Impact
- Water consumption per AI prompt.
- Electricity consumption per prompt.
- Carbon emissions per prompt.
- Direct and indirect water consumption.
- Source-backed statistics.

## Section 2: Environmental Calculator
- Daily AI prompt input.
- Adjustable environmental assumptions.
- Daily and annual projections.
- Animated water drop transforming into a container.
- Display units and methodology.

## Section 3: Human Creativity
- Animated documentary.
- Research about AI and creativity.
- Human versus AI idea examples.
- Idea diversity and cognitive offloading.
- Balanced scientific explanations.

## Section 4: Research
- Academic references.
- Research publication dates.
- Methodology.
- Evidence limitations.
- Original source links.

## Technical Preferences
- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Motion
- Recharts
- Static JSON research data

## Constraints
- Zero-budget MVP.
- No paid APIs.
- No login system.
- No database.
- No fabricated statistics.
- No unsupported scientific claims.
- No unnecessary personal data collection.

## Quality Requirements
- Responsive design.
- Accessible navigation.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Fast loading.
- Correct calculations.
- Mobile-friendly animations.
- Verified research citations.

## Development Rules
1. Work in milestones.
2. Create an implementation plan before coding.
3. Never invent research data.
4. Keep calculations separate from UI components.
5. Test every major feature.
6. Review the browser appearance after implementation.
7. Do not proceed to the next milestone without reviewing
   the current milestone.
